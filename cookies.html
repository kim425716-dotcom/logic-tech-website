Cookie Governance

Cookie Policy

Information regarding cookies, session storage, and security tokens on the Logic Tech website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies and local browser storage are small pieces of encrypted data stored directly on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They enable session continuity, remember your preferences (like light/dark theme), and guard against cross-site request forgery.

2. Cookies & Storage We Use

Strictly Necessary (Authentication & Security) HttpOnly, SameSite-protected cookies (e.g. lt_session) that maintain secure user authentication sessions without exposing credentials to client scripts.
Functional Preferences Client storage keys (e.g. theme) that persist your preferred interface mode (Dark/Light mode) across browser visits.
Performance & Diagnostics Aggregated telemetry cookies that detect site performance bottlenecks and network latency without tracking individual user identities.

3. Third-Party Tracking

Logic Tech does not sell advertising space or utilize third-party data tracking brokers. We only embed essential services such as Google Fonts and FontAwesome for UI rendering.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can adjust your browser settings to decline or clear cookies at any time. Note that disabling strictly necessary authentication cookies will prevent you from signing in to the client dashboard.
