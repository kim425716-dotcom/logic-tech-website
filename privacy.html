Data Protection & Governance

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Your privacy and corporate confidentiality are foundational to our client partnerships.

1. Overview & Scope

Logic Tech ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and data integrity of all users, clients, and partners. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, process, and safeguard information gathered through our website, client dashboards, API services, and consulting engagements.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver high-quality technology solutions and support:

  • Contact & Identity Data: Name, work email address, company name, phone number, and professional role provided when submitting inquiries or creating an account.
  • Project & Technical Specifications: Project briefs, architecture requirements, budget estimates, and mutual non-disclosure preferences.
  • Authentication & Session Data: Securely salted and scrypt-hashed credentials, cryptographic session identifiers, and role-based access tokens.
  • Technical Log Data: IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, and page interaction timestamps for security monitoring and rate limiting.

3. How We Use Your Data

We utilize collected data strictly for the following purposes:

  • Providing custom software engineering, cloud architecture, and cybersecurity services.
  • Facilitating client portal access, project progress monitoring, and support ticketing.
  • Executing and enforcing Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) and Master Services Agreements (MSAs).
  • Protecting our infrastructure against malicious attacks, unauthorized access, and automated abuse.

4. Data Confidentiality & Client IP

We treat all proprietary source code, software architectures, database schemas, and business algorithms shared during consultations or project lifecycles as strictly confidential. We do not sell, rent, or monetize client data under any circumstances.

5. Security Architecture

Our platform employs defense-in-depth principles: TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, secure HttpOnly SameSite session cookies, rate-limiting algorithms, and regular penetration audits.

6. Data Subject Rights (GDPR & Kenya Data Protection Act 2019)

You have the right to access, rectify, port, or request the erasure of your personal records at any time. To exercise your rights, please reach out to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@logictech.co.ke.
